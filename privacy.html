Privacy Policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

E Market Hub LLC (“E Market Hub,” “we,” “us”) is an independent authorized retailer of residential internet service. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us by phone or email — such as your name, service address, phone number, email address and the details a provider needs to check availability or place your order.
  • Call information — the number you call from and the date, time and length of the call. Calls may be monitored or recorded for quality and training where permitted by law.
  • Basic technical information — like most websites, our hosting provider may log your IP address, browser type and pages visited to operate and secure the site.

How we use information

  • To check which services are available at your address and explain your options.
  • To place and follow up on orders you request with the provider you choose.
  • To answer your questions and keep records of our communications.
  • To comply with law and with the requirements of the providers we represent.

How we share information

We share your information with the internet provider you choose, and with that provider’s authorized sales-program administrators, only as needed to check availability and place your order. We may also share information with service vendors who help us run our business (such as phone and email services) under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us not to contact you again, or ask about the information we hold about you, by calling (929) 644-9889 or emailing info@emarkethubllc.com. Once an order is placed, the provider’s own privacy policy governs the information it holds about your service.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed.
