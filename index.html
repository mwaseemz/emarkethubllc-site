Internet options

Which kind of internet fits your home?

Most addresses have more than one choice. Here’s how the main types compare — we’ll tell you which ones reach yours.

Fiber internet

Data travels as light over fiber-optic lines, giving fast downloads, equally fast uploads and a steady connection.

Great for: busy households, working from home, gaming and 4K streaming.

High-speed internet

Wired service over the local phone network or another fixed line — a dependable choice where fiber hasn’t arrived yet.

Great for: browsing, email, video calls and everyday streaming.

Satellite internet

Service from satellites in orbit, available at almost any address with a clear view of the sky — even beyond wired networks.

Great for: rural and remote homes without wired options.

FAQ

Common questions

Is E Market Hub an internet provider?

No. E Market Hub LLC is an independent authorized retailer — a sales company. The provider you choose supplies, installs, bills and supports your service under its own terms.

Does it cost anything to use E Market Hub?

No. There is no charge for our help. You pay only the provider for the service you order, at the provider’s own price.

Which providers can you check for my address?

We check every provider we’re authorized to sell, including the providers shown on this page. Which of them can serve you depends on your exact service address, so we check it on the call.

How long does the call take?

An availability check usually takes just a few minutes. If you decide to order, we complete the order with you on the same call.

Who installs my service?

The provider. After your order is placed, the provider confirms your installation or activation details and sets up your service.

What information will I need?

Your service address and contact details. To place an order, providers also ask for information such as your name, phone and email, and some providers verify identity or credit before activating service.

I already have service. Can you help with my bill or an outage?

We sell new service only and can’t access existing accounts. For billing, outages or technical support, please contact your provider directly using the number on your bill or the provider’s official website.
